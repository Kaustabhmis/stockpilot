Content Policy

Last updated: — · Version 1.0

This policy forms part of the Terms of Use. It explains what you may post, where it belongs, and what is never permitted.

Two places, two standards

Discovery feedPrivate gallery
Who sees itEveryone, including signed-out visitors via shared linksThe creator and their active subscribers only
StandardSafe for a public timelineArtistic and partial nudity permitted
Link previewsShow the imageReveal only that a post exists — never its contents

Putting mature work in the public feed is a policy breach even though the same work would be perfectly acceptable in a private gallery. The distinction is about who did not choose to see it.

Permitted in private galleries

  • Artistic, fine-art and figure nudity
  • Partial nudity, implied nudity, lingerie, swimwear
  • Boudoir, glamour and fashion photography
  • Body-positive and non-sexualised nude work
  • Behind-the-scenes material from any of the above

Never permitted, anywhere

Anything sexual involving a minor — real, drawn, generated, or a person who appears to be a minor. There is no artistic exemption, no context that makes it acceptable, and no warning before action. Accounts are closed permanently, content is preserved as evidence, and reports are made to the authorities as required under the POCSO Act and the IT Act.

  • Any identifiable person who has not consented, in writing, to both the creation and the publication of the content
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery, hidden-camera material, or sexual deepfakes of real people
  • Coercion, trafficking, or the sale of sexual services
  • Bestiality, incest, necrophilia, sexualised violence
  • Content obscene under sections 67, 67A and 67B of the Information Technology Act, 2000
  • Gore, self-harm promotion, terrorist or extremist material
  • Hate speech or targeted harassment
  • Work you do not own or have no right to publish
  • Fraud, malware, phishing, or attempts to take payment off-platform
  • Another person's private information

Consent and records

If anyone other than you appears in your content, you must hold:

  • a signed model release covering publication on this platform;
  • proof that every person shown was 18 or over at the time;
  • records kept for at least [N] years.

We may request these at any time and expect them within [N] days. Content stays down until they are produced.

Reporting

Report anything that breaches this policy to [REPORT EMAIL]. Include the link and what is wrong. Reports of non-consensual intimate imagery or content involving minors are prioritised and actioned within 24 hours.

You may also contact the Grievance Officer named in the Terms of Use.

A note on where the line sits

"Artistic nudity" and "obscenity" are not neatly separable, and the boundary differs by jurisdiction. We apply this policy in good faith and will sometimes make judgement calls you disagree with. If a decision looks wrong, appeal it — but understand that we err toward removal where a real person's safety, consent or age is uncertain.
