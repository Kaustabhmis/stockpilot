Security

  • Passwords are hashed with scrypt, a memory-hard function. We cannot read them, and neither can anyone who steals the database.
  • Sessions are opaque tokens that expire, and are revoked immediately when an account is suspended. They are held separately from the database, not in the spreadsheet.
  • Private media is served only through short-lived links tied to the individual viewer, so a subscriber cannot hand a working link to a non-subscriber.
  • Traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.
