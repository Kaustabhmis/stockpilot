Cancellation

Subscriptions do not auto-renew. Access simply ends 30 days after purchase, so there is nothing to cancel to avoid a future charge. You may end a subscription early from the creator's profile; doing so stops access immediately and does not itself trigger a refund.

When we refund

SituationOutcome
Duplicate or unauthorised chargeFull refund

Creator's account closed within [N] days of your purchasePro-rata refund

Changed your mind after using the accessGenerally no refund

Nothing here removes rights you have under consumer law that cannot be contracted away.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before raising a chargeback — it is usually faster. Where a chargeback is raised on a payment already paid out to a creator, we may recover the amount from that creator's future earnings.
