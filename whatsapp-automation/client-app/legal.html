Terms of Service

  • We may suspend accounts that send spam, illegal or misleading content, without refund.

Privacy Policy

  • We collect your business details (name, email, phone, website, GSTIN) to provide the service and issue receipts.
  • Customer lists and campaign content you upload are used only to deliver your campaigns and reports. We do not sell or share them.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store card or bank details.
  • Data is stored with Google Workspace / Google Cloud services. You can request export or deletion of your data by contacting us.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • You can cancel at any time; your plan stays active until the end of the paid period and is not renewed automatically.
  • Refunds: if we cannot activate your service within 7 days of payment, you may request a full refund. Once messages have been sent in a billing period, that period's fee is non-refundable.
  • Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.

Service Delivery

This is a digital service. Your account is activated online immediately after successful payment; access details are shown on screen and emailed to you. Setup of a dedicated WhatsApp number is completed within 2 working days.
