Fair by design

A score your team will believe

Most performance software produces a number nobody can argue with because nobody can see where it came from. Every figure here traces back to a named task, a date and a person.

The formula

Score = how well you delivered × how much you delivered

On time 45%, right first time 30%, nothing left late 25% — each task weighted by its priority. Ten jobs with seven on time beats one easy job done well, because the rate is credited against the load actually carried. A working day late costs 10 points on that task; a send-back costs 25.

Questions

Questions buyers ask

How long does it take to set up?

An afternoon. Create the workspace, add your people with their manager, and assign the first jobs. There is nothing to install — it runs in the browser on a computer or a phone.

Will my team actually use it?

Staff see only their own work, what to do first, and their own score, with reminders by email. A printable manual with a picture of every screen comes with it, and Help inside the app opens on whatever page you are on.

Is our data safe? Can other companies see it?

Every company gets its own private workspace. Nothing is shared between workspaces and nothing is public. Passwords are stored as secure hashes, and every change is recorded with a name and a time.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

If you choose a plan, nothing changes. If you do not, the workspace moves to the Free plan — 5 people and 100 tasks a month. Nothing is deleted and everybody can still sign in.

Do we get a GST invoice?

Yes. Fill in your GSTIN under Plans before paying, and a GST invoice is emailed the moment the payment goes through. Every invoice stays under Plans → Invoices.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, in any phone browser. The board, priority list, meetings and scores all work on a small screen.

Can we change plans later?

Yes. Move up as your team grows; renewing early adds to the time you already have rather than replacing it.

How is the score kept fair?

Lateness is counted in working days, approved leave is left out, a slow checker never counts against the doer, and work handed in is never counted as late. Anyone can open “See why” and check every number.
