A score your team will believe

Most performance software produces a number nobody can argue with because nobody can see where it came from. Every figure here traces back to a named task, a date and a person.

The formula

Score = how well you delivered × how much you delivered

A rate on its own is blind to volume. Ten jobs with seven delivered on time has to beat one easy job delivered — so the rate is credited against the load actually carried, weighted by priority. Work in hand counts, and so do the approvals you clear for other people.

Managers are measured too

Sit on an approval and you lose score for it, counted in working days from the moment it reached your desk.

Leave is never counted as lateness

Weekends, company holidays and that person's own approved leave are excluded everywhere a day is counted.

Nobody wears somebody else's slip

If the stage before yours overran, your deadline moves with it. You get back the window the plan gave you.

0 to 100, always

Everybody starts at zero and earns it. Nothing — recognition included — takes anyone past a hundred.
