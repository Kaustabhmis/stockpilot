# Domebox on LinkedIn — 4-week launch plan, posts and images

## How LinkedIn works for a product like Domebox

- **Post from your personal profile first, then reshare from the Domebox company page.**
  LinkedIn shows people's posts far more widely than company pages. Owners buy from a
  founder they can see.
- **Put the link in the first comment, not in the post.** Posts with links get shown less.
  Write "Link in the first comment", then add `https://www.domebox.in` as the first comment.
- **Best times (IST):** Tuesday to Thursday, 8:30–10:30 AM.
- **The first hour decides how far a post travels.** Reply to every comment within the
  hour, and spend 15 minutes before and after posting commenting on other owners' posts.
- **2–3 posts a week is enough.** Being consistent beats posting a lot.
- **Use 3–5 hashtags at the end**, not in the middle of sentences.

## What to avoid

- **Don't make up numbers, customers or testimonials.** LinkedIn audiences spot it, and
  it breaks trust with exactly the owners you want.
- **Don't use Microsoft's logo.** Name the Founders Hub in words only.
- **Don't post the same image on the same day as Facebook.** Leave a few days between.
- **Don't argue in the comments.** Thank people and answer the question.

## What to track each week

| Measure | Where |
|---|---|
| Impressions, reactions, comments | LinkedIn post analytics |
| Profile views and new followers | LinkedIn dashboard |
| Visits from LinkedIn | Netlify analytics → referrers |
| Free trials started | Master sheet → Directory (new rows) |

After 4 weeks, keep the format that brought the most **comments and trials**, not likes,
and do more of it.
