Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry or site-visit form we collect the details you provide: name, mobile number, email address (optional), property preferences (project, configuration, budget, location), preferred visit date and your message. We also record the page you enquired from and any campaign parameters in the link (for example utm_source) so we know which advertisements are useful.

How we use it

Your information is used only to respond to your enquiry, arrange site visits, share project documents and price lists, and send updates about projects you showed interest in. With your consent we may contact you by call, SMS, email or WhatsApp.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share it with our banking or legal partners only when you ask us to (for example for home-loan assistance), or where required by law.

Third-party services

This website uses Google Maps to display project locations and may load fonts and scripts from trusted content delivery networks. These providers may collect technical data such as your IP address in line with their own privacy policies.
