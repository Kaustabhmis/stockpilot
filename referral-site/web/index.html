Questions people ask

Do I need a licence or a RERA registration?

No. You are referring a buyer to us, not brokering the deal yourself. We do the selling and we hold the brokerage agreement with the seller.

What if two people refer the same buyer?

The first referral wins. Whoever submits a buyer's mobile number first owns that buyer for 90 days. A later referral for the same number is still recorded — marked duplicate — so if there is any dispute we can see exactly who came first and when.

When exactly do I get paid?

After registration, not after booking. A booking can fall through; a registration cannot. Once the property is registered in the buyer's name, your commission becomes payable and is released within 15 days.

Is anything deducted?

TDS at 2% is deducted at source on commission, as the law requires. The calculator above already shows you the net amount.

Is there a target or a joining fee?

Neither. Refer one buyer a year or twenty, it works the same way, and joining is free.

What does the buyer pay?

Nothing to you. Our brokerage comes from the seller side, so your referral costs the buyer nothing extra.
