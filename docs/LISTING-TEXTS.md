### Long (about 300 words — SoftwareSuggest, Techjockey, G2, Capterra, SaaSworthy, SourceForge)
Domebox is task, team and performance management software built for Indian MSMEs: factories, trading houses and service firms of 5 to 300 people.

**Every job has an owner.** Each task gets one owner, a due date and a priority, and it is closed only when the person who asked for it verifies it, with an optional photo as proof. Work moves across a shared board (To do, In progress, For review, Verified). Approvals follow the reporting line. Blocked work shows what it is waiting for. Recurring jobs such as calibration, maintenance and audits create themselves on schedule, and multi-stage projects give each stage its own owner.

**Goals that the whole company can see moving.** The owner sets company goals; each head of department sets goals for their own team that serve them, with a target figure such as "rejections from 4% to 1.5%". Each week the owner enters the figure, and Domebox says whether the goal is on track, at risk or off track, and why. The Admin sees every department's progress in one review.

**Performance you can check.** Each person's monthly score comes from their own task record: on time, right first time, nothing left late, weighted by priority and volume. Anyone can open "See why" and trace every number to a task and a date. KRAs, KPIs and appraisals sit next to the work they measure, with a leaderboard and recognition points.

**Meetings that become work.** Weekly reviews run in eight timed segments; each department can hold its own, visible only to the people invited. Any point can be turned into a task in one click.

Also included: Excel import of the whole team, email reminders on each company's own working days and holidays, a monthly report emailed to the owner, an org chart and an activity log.

Free for 5 users. Paid plans from ₹999 a month for 10 users, the whole product in every plan. 14-day free trial, no card.
