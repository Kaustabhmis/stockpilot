## 1. Before you start (10 minutes, once)

1. **Use one email for all three sites:** info@biscsindia.com.
   G2 and Capterra check that you work for the vendor. Because the email
   (biscsindia.com) differs from the product site (domebox.in), they may ask
   for proof. Keep this line ready:
   *"Domebox is a product of BISCS India. biscsindia.com is our company domain;
   domebox.in is the product site. See the footer of www.domebox.in."*
   If they still refuse, create info@domebox.in (Zoho Mail has a free plan)
   and use that instead.
2. **Make sure the website is live** with the latest upload (the AI Overview
   update), because reviewers open it.
3. **Never write or buy reviews.** All three sites remove fake reviews and can
   remove the listing. Ask real users instead (section 6).

### Long description (about 250 words)
Domebox is task, team and performance management software for Indian MSMEs:
factories, trading houses and service firms of 5 to 300 people.

Every job gets one owner and a due date, and it is closed only when the person
who asked for it verifies it. Work moves across a shared board (To do, In
progress, For review, Verified). Approvals follow the reporting line. Recurring
jobs such as calibration, maintenance and audits create themselves on schedule.
Multi-stage projects give each stage its own owner and deadline.

Each person's monthly performance score is built from their own task record:
how well they delivered (on time 45%, right first time 30%, nothing left late
25%, weighted by priority) multiplied by how much they delivered. Managers are
scored on the approvals they hold. Anyone can open "See why" and trace every
number to a task and a date.

KRAs, KPIs and appraisals sit in the same place as the work they measure. There
are also goals, a leaderboard, a weekly meeting module, an org chart that builds
itself, and email reminders at times each company sets, on its own working days
and holidays.

It runs in any web browser, with nothing to install. Every company gets its own
private workspace, and every change is recorded with a name and a time.

Pricing is in INR. It is free for 5 users. Paid plans start at ₹999 a month for
10 users and include the whole product. There is a 14-day free trial with no
card.

## 6. Getting the first reviews (what makes a listing count)

A listing with no reviews is barely shown. Aim for **10 genuine reviews in the
first month**:

1. Ask the people who use Domebox every day (customers and pilot users, never
   your own staff or family, whom the sites treat as fake reviews) to review on **one site at a time**,
   G2 first.
2. Use the review link each site gives you in the vendor portal, not a
   general link.
3. Message to send (WhatsApp or email):

   > Hi [Name], you have been using Domebox for [team / tasks]. Could you spend
   > 5 minutes writing an honest review on G2? Good or bad, both help us.
   > [review link]. Thank you! Kaustabh, BISCS India

4. **Don't** offer money or gifts unless the site's own programme does it
   (G2 and Capterra sometimes run gift-card campaigns through their portal;
   those are allowed). **Don't** write reviews yourself or ask staff to
   pretend to be customers.
